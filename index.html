03 · How it works

How Syph works in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect your tools

    Sign in once to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Odoo CRM, WhatsApp and Telegram. For each tool, you decide whether employees can only read or can also make changes.

  2. 02

    Hire an AI employee

    Pick a role like Sales, Marketing or Executive assistant, or write your own. Give it a mission, a personality and a schedule. It picks up your company's background from the shared Brain, so you don't have to explain it twice.

  3. 03

    Approve what matters

    Your employee works on its own every day. Anything that sends, spends or commits you to something waits in Needs you, and you approve it with one tap on the web or your iPhone.

08 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something else?

What exactly is an “AI employee”?

A persistent agent with a name, a role, a mission and access to the tools you choose. It wakes up on its schedule, reads what's new, does the work, and remembers your preferences, for example “never negotiate below €90k”.

Can it do things without my permission?

Only what you allow. You choose read or write access per tool and an autonomy level per employee. Actions that leave your workspace, such as sending an email, wait for your approval in Needs you. You can pause one employee, or all of them, at any time.

Which tools does Syph connect to?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, Odoo CRM, WhatsApp, Telegram, web research and on-brand slide decks, with more on the way. Business plans can add custom integrations.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You connect tools by signing in, and you brief employees in plain language, the same way you'd brief a new hire.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Syph runs on the web and on iPhone, so you can chat with your team and approve actions from anywhere.

How does billing work?

Solo and Team are monthly subscriptions paid by card. Business is tailored to your team. Talk to sales and we'll work out the right size with you.
